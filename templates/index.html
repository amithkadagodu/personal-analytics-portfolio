About
A bit more about my path
  • Background
  • What I do
  • Why Data Science

I started out as a Site Civil Engineer, managing site operations, quantity estimation, and project coordination. Looking for more analytical work, I moved into content and data analyst roles at Thomson Reuters' FindLaw platform, and I'm now completing an MSc in Data Science at Manipal Academy of Higher Education.

I manage the end-to-end administration and data quality of legal directory products across Thomson Reuters' FindLaw platform — evaluating and validating digital data products for compliance and accuracy at scale.

I'm drawn to data science because it turns operational data into decisions — building dashboards, running statistical analysis, and modeling predictions, rather than just maintaining data.
